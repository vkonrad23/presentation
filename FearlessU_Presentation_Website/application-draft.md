## About us
FearlessU is developing an AI-assisted behavioral-health platform that helps people practice personalized, evidence-aligned cognitive behavioral skills in everyday moments of fear and avoidance. The product is designed to support, not replace, licensed care. It combines structured check-ins, clinician-reviewed skills, small behavior-change exercises, progress reflection, and clear escalation pathways.

## Primary product or service
FearlessU converts a user’s goal and situation into a short, structured “courage experiment.” The AI personalizes language, pacing, and sequencing within a controlled content library, while safety rules limit scope, identify higher-risk interactions, and direct users toward professional or emergency resources where appropriate. ## Problem
Fear can produce avoidance at the exact moment a person needs a practical next step. Existing support may be delayed, generic, difficult to access, or disconnected from daily context. FearlessU is designed to close the gap between insight and action through brief, structured skills practice while preserving clear clinical boundaries.

## 60-second pitch
Fear should not decide the size of a life. Everyday avoidance can shrink work, relationships, and health, yet support is often generic, delayed, or disconnected from the moment action is needed. FearlessU is building an AI-assisted behavioral-health platform that turns evidence-aligned, clinician-reviewed CBT skills into personalized and measurable courage experiments. Users check in, receive one small practice, compare feared predictions with real outcomes, and learn from progress over time. The system is designed with strict scope controls, transparent AI, and pathways to professional or emergency support. With Mayo Clinic and ASU, we want to validate the clinical workflow, establish the evidence and safety plan, and prove a scalable route into care. FearlessU: one fear, one step, more life.
