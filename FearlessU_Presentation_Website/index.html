DILIGENCE

Questions we expect.

Does FearlessU replace a therapist?+

No. The product is presented as an AI-assisted skills and navigation platform. It should not diagnose, prescribe, or substitute for licensed care. Its exact intended use must be refined with clinical and regulatory guidance.

Does the AI invent new CBT treatments?+

Not autonomously. A safer approach is to personalize delivery and sequencing within a clinician-reviewed library. Any genuinely new therapeutic technique would require expert design, risk assessment, and rigorous validation before use or claims.

What is the initial beachhead market?+

The founder should select one segment based on interviews and purchasing evidence. A credible application is stronger with one specific initial workflow than a claim to solve every psychological problem.

What is the moat?+

The proposed moat combines safety architecture, proprietary outcome-linked personalization, clinically reviewed content operations, workflow integration, and evidence generated with trusted partners.

ELEVATOR PITCH SCRIPT

Fear should not decide the size of a life.

Millions of everyday moments are shaped by avoidance, yet support is often generic, delayed, or disconnected from the moment action is needed. FearlessU is building an AI-assisted behavioral-health platform that turns evidence-aligned CBT skills into personalized, measurable courage experiments.

Users check in, receive one small clinician-reviewed practice, compare feared predictions with real outcomes, and learn from progress over time. The system is designed with strict scope controls, transparent AI, and pathways to professional or emergency support.

With Mayo Clinic and ASU, we want to validate the workflow, establish the evidence and safety plan, and prove a scalable route into care. FearlessU: one fear, one step, more life.
