Privacy principles

This page is a presentation placeholder, not a final legal policy. Obtain qualified privacy and legal review before collecting personal or health information.

Data minimization

Collect only the information required for a defined user benefit, document purpose, and provide clear retention and deletion controls.

Security

Plan for encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access, audit logging, incident response, secure development, and vendor diligence.

Health information

Before launch, determine which laws and contractual obligations apply in each market and workflow. Do not claim HIPAA compliance until verified.

AI transparency

Tell users when they interact with AI, explain the system's role and limitations, and provide a route to human support.
